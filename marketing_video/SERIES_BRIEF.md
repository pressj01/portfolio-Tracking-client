# Portfolio Tracker Video Series Brief

This file is the standing brief for every Portfolio Tracker marketing video.

## Requirements that apply to every video

- Use a natural, human-sounding voice. Do not use a robotic or obviously synthetic delivery.
- Protect the customer account total in every screen capture by blurring only that total.
- Other portfolio, holding, performance, income, yield, risk, and comparison numbers may remain visible.
- Show screens only after their data, charts, and layout are fully loaded. Never use a loading, skeleton, partially rendered, or error state in a finished video.
- Keep the tone clear, confident, conversational, and educational. Avoid hype and financial promises.
- Include the disclosure: "For informational purposes only. Not financial advice."
- Produce the primary video at 1920 by 1080 with an H.264 video track and AAC audio.

## Voice baseline

The overview uses the warm, conversational `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` voice at a slightly relaxed rate (`-4%`) and pitch (`-2Hz`). Use this as the series baseline unless a later video has a deliberate reason to change voices. Always listen for natural pacing before approving a final export.

## Series structure

1. **Complete site overview**: a high-level tour that shows the breadth of the application and the way its major workflows connect. It should not teach every control.
2. **Detailed screen videos**: one video per screen or closely related workflow, with enough detail for a customer to understand the controls, outputs, and common use cases.

## Overview video coverage

The overview should represent all major areas without attempting to show every page:

- Portfolio dashboard and action center
- Holdings, income tracking, and calendars
- Security research, screeners, checklists, and comparisons
- Portfolio analytics, diversification, and risk
- Options dashboard, trades, probability tools, strategy lab, and scanners
- Retirement, income, withdrawal, and rebalancing planning
- Tax reporting and tax-loss harvesting
- Import, export, Split View, navigation customization, settings, and help
